Logo Stamper

Put your logo on hundreds of photos — one click each.

Starting the program

  1. Unzip the folder Unzip LogoStamper-windows.zip somewhere easy, like your Desktop. Unzip it properly first — do not run it from inside the zip preview window.
  2. Double-click “START HERE - Logo Stamper” A black window opens and your web browser opens automatically.
  3. Leave the black window alone That window is the program. Keep it open while you work. Closing it closes the program. You do the actual work in the browser.
If Windows shows a blue “Windows protected your PC” box Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the program is not code-signed, not because anything is wrong with it.
Nothing is uploaded anywhere Everything happens on this computer. The program works with the internet switched off. Your photos never leave the machine.

Setting up a batch

  1. Choose the folder with your photos Click Choose folder…. A normal Windows window opens — you can paste a path in the address bar at the top. Open the folder you want, then click Open.
  2. Choose your logo — or several A PNG with a transparent background works best.
    To use more than one logo you can either hold Ctrl and click several files in the window, or simply press “Add logo…” again for each one — they add up. Each logo appears in a numbered list underneath, and the × next to any of them removes it.
  3. Choose where finished photos go — or skip it Leave it alone and the program makes a new folder next to your photos, named after it with _stamped on the end.
  4. Press Start
Your original photos are never touched Finished copies are written as photo_stamped.jpg in the output folder. The originals stay exactly as they were.

Placing the logo

A photo fills the screen and your logo follows the mouse. Click where you want it. The photo is saved and the next one appears immediately — you do not press Save, and you do not wait.

Made a mistake? Click again on the same photo to move the logo, or press Backspace to go back to the previous photo.

The fast way: number keys

The number keys drop the logo into nine fixed spots, laid out like a numpad:

7top left
8top centre
9top right
4left
5centre
6right
1bottom left
2bottom centre
3bottom right

This is much quicker than aiming with the mouse. Most people end up using 9 or 3 for nearly every photo, and Space to repeat the previous position.

Using more than one logo

If you picked several logos, small pictures of them appear in the toolbar at the top. The one with the blue outline is the one you are placing now.

  • Click (or press a number) to place the highlighted logo.
  • The program stays on the same photo and moves to your next logo.
  • Once every logo is placed, it moves on to the next photo.
  • A green ✓ on a logo means it is already placed on this photo.

Tab switches logo by hand. Delete removes the current logo from this photo. Space copies all the logo positions from the previous photo at once.

Size and opacity

Size slider Changes the logo size on every photo. Photos you already finished are redone automatically — you do not need to start over.
Opacity slider Makes the logo more see-through, for a subtle watermark. Also applies to every photo and redoes finished ones.
+ and − Same as the Size slider, from the keyboard.
Shift + mouse wheel Changes the size for this one photo only, leaving the rest alone.
Why the logo looks the right size on every photo The size is measured against the whole photo, not its width. That way the logo carries the same visual weight on a wide landscape shot and a tall portrait one, even when your photos are all different sizes.

Stopping and coming back later

You can stop at any time — there is no Save button because everything is saved as you go. Close the browser and the black window whenever you like.

To carry on, start the program again and choose the same photo folder. It remembers every photo you already did and takes you straight to the first one you have not done yet. This also means nothing is lost if the computer crashes or the power goes out.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
The browser did not open Look in the black window — it shows an address like http://127.0.0.1:8753/. Type that into your browser.
The “Choose folder” window did not appear It may have opened behind the browser — check the taskbar. If it truly does not work, open “The Choose buttons did not work” at the bottom of the first screen and type the folder path instead.
A red bar appears at the bottom Something went wrong inside the program. Note the message and send it to your developer.
A photo would not save The finish screen lists any photos that failed — usually a damaged file. Everything else still completes normally.
You want to start a folder over Delete the hidden .logostamper folder inside your photo folder, and delete the _stamped output folder.

About quality

  • Photos are never shrunk — the finished file is the same size as the original.
  • JPEGs are saved at very high quality with no colour subsampling.
  • Camera information (EXIF) and the colour profile are carried across.
  • Photos taken sideways keep the right orientation.
