    Keyboard shortcuts

    Click on the photo
    Put the logo there and go to the next photo
    1 – 9
    Nine fixed spots, laid out like a numpad:
    7 8 9 = top row · 4 5 6 = middle · 1 2 3 = bottom
    Space
    Use the same spot as the previous photo
    S
    Skip this photo (no logo)
    Backspace
    Go back one photo to redo it
    ← →
    Move between photos without changing anything
    Tab
    Switch to the next logo (when you chose more than one)
    Delete
    Remove the current logo from this photo
    + / −
    Make the logo bigger or smaller (all photos)
    Shift + scroll
    Change the size for this one photo only
    Esc
    Close this box
